# Financial Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

The Financial Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It focuses on analyzing sales performance, profitability, product performance, customer segments, and monthly sales trends.

The dashboard transforms financial data into meaningful business insights, enabling users to evaluate performance across different countries, products, customer segments, and time periods.

It provides a centralized view of key performance indicators (KPIs) with interactive Country and Year filters for dynamic analysis.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify the most profitable products.
- Compare sales performance across different countries.
- Evaluate revenue contribution by customer segment.
- Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Examine sales distribution across discount bands.
- Support data-driven business decision-making.

## Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

### 1. Sales by Country
- Compares sales performance across different countries.
- Identifies the highest-performing geographical markets.

### 2. Sales by Segment
- Displays revenue contribution from different customer segments.
- Identifies the customer segments generating the highest sales.

### 3. Sales by Year and Month
- Tracks sales performance over time.
- Highlights monthly sales peaks and fluctuations.

### 4. Profit by Product
- Compares profit generated by individual products.
- Identifies the most and least profitable products.

### 5. Sale Price by Discount Band
- Displays sales price distribution across different discount categories.
- Helps evaluate discount-related sales patterns.

### 6. Sales by Product
- Compares total sales generated by individual products.
- Identifies products with the highest revenue contribution.

### 7. Sales and Profit by Month
- Compares monthly sales and profit performance.
- Helps identify revenue and profitability trends.

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Overall Business Performance
- Total sales reached **118.73M**.
- Total profit amounted to **16.89M**.
- Overall profit margin was **14.23%**.
- Total units sold reached **1.13M**.

### 2. Country Performance
- United States and Canada recorded approximately 25M in sales each.
- France and Germany generated approximately 24M each.
- Mexico recorded approximately 21M in sales.

**Insight:** Sales performance is relatively balanced across the five countries, with the United States and Canada leading.

### 3. Customer Segment Performance
- Government: 52.5M (44.22%)
- Small Business: 42.43M (35.74%)
- Enterprise: 19.61M (16.52%)
- Midmarket: 2.38M (2.01%)

**Insight:** Government represents the largest customer segment, contributing approximately 44.22% of total sales.

### 4. Product Profitability
- Paseo generated the highest profit at approximately 4.8M.
- VTT and Amarilla were the next strongest performers.
- Carretera recorded the lowest profit among the displayed products.

**Insight:** Paseo is the leading product in terms of profitability.

### 5. Discount Analysis
- High Discount: 34.16%
- Medium Discount: 34.09%
- Low Discount: 25.85%
- No Discount: 5.91%

**Insight:** High and Medium discount categories account for the largest shares of the displayed sales price distribution.

### 6. Monthly Sales Trends
- Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the displayed period.
- Several significant sales peaks are visible.
- Monthly sales and profit movements can be compared to identify performance changes.

**Insight:** Monitoring monthly performance can help identify periods of strong and weak business activity.
